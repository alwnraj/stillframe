## Design Context

### Users
Letterboxd users reading the current public RSS movie window by entering a username, without uploads or terminal commands. Reliability takes priority over collecting extra pages. Data is used only for the current lookup and is not retained.

### Brand Personality
Simple, clear, film-focused. Make collection status, coverage and transient data lifetime easy to understand.

### Aesthetic Direction
One serif voice (Georgia; italic for headings and film titles), a plain left-aligned layout in the spirit of radius.earendil.com, deep red with cream text and a gold button, and the results on a 35mm film strip. A hand-made finish borrowed from painted cel backgrounds (fofr.ai's typhoon greenhouse): a soft gouache wash on the red, paper cards with slightly uneven edges and a tilt, inked lines with a little tremor, a hand-drawn gold underline, and a gentle 35mm glow. The results strip itself stays conservative in material (clean cream cards, no tilt or rough edges) and shows no numbers at all: pictures and words only, with exact counts kept for screen readers, and the doodle band runs straight and slow without flicker. Stats under the strip are never dashboard charts: each is drawn as a piece of cinema (glyph postcard in the spirit of Dear Data, marquee bulbs, seating chart, laurels, clapperboards, a double-feature poster, a ticket), on theme, static, and always with a plain-text reading for screen readers. Keep it quiet: no glitch effects, no torn-paper extremes, all textures are inline SVG so the strict CSP holds. No costume copy (tickets, receipts, puns) and no stacked effects; motion should feel alive and hand-animated: line boil at 8 frames a second on ink and paper edges, a running film band of hand-inked doodles, a pointer-following projector light with lamp flicker and grain, twinkling dust, entrances that rise, develop and deal in, plus the countdown leader. Everything stops under reduced motion, and nothing may cause sideways scrolling or move a clickable control continuously. Motion stops under reduced motion. Keep text contrast high on the dark background.

### Design Principles
- Keep the username form the primary action.
- Never mention RSS or feeds on the page (user's direction). Stay honest without it: say "recent films" and "not your full history", and never label sample ratings or entries as full account history.
- Show real results and explicit failures without fabricated content. Distinguish first-page coverage from a full collection.
- Keep loading, keyboard focus, mobile layouts and the Clear now action accessible. Do not introduce exports, history or saved results.

The finish is mixed media on textured paper: impasto gouache across the red, paper tooth under the cream cards, waxy crayon on headings and stars, and streaky coloured pencil on drawn pieces. Texture is static SVG filters and data backgrounds; it adds no motion, and body text and small labels stay unfiltered for legibility.
